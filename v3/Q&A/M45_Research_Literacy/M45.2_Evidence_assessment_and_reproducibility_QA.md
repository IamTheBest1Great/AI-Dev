## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q37. [Interview] Explain evidence assessment and reproducibility to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Evidence assessment and reproducibility addresses the need to distinguish credible results from benchmark hype. I would begin with a bounded design for a technical team evaluating new agent research, then specify contracts for Experimental design and Baselines. I would measure reproducible improvement on representative local tests, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q38. [Interview] How would you design Experimental design from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. Plan variables, baselines, sampling and criteria before collecting results to avoid post-hoc metric selection. Then I would create at least a happy-path and an unauthorized/invalid-input test.


**Q39. [Interview] How do Baselines and Ablations interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Current simple methods used as a reference, with the same dataset, budget and measurement conditions as the proposed improvement. Controlled removal or alteration of one component to estimate its contribution to measured behavior. I would connect them only through a typed handoff with provenance and an explicit postcondition, rather than allowing hidden state coupling.


**Q40. [Interview] What would you do if Reproducibility fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure reproducible improvement on representative local tests.


**Q41. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for evidence assessment and reproducibility?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q42. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M45.2?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the research evidence card and reproducibility checklist, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
