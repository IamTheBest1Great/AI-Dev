## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q37. [Interview] Explain channel adapters to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Channel adapters addresses the need to convert inbound messages into secure, idempotent tasks. I would begin with a bounded design for a multi-channel support bot with webhooks and handoff, then specify contracts for Telegram and Slack. I would measure successful delivery and duplicate-trigger suppression, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure: a messaging provider retries a webhook and the bot creates two tickets.


**Q38. [Interview] How would you design Telegram from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. In this part of a multi-channel support bot with webhooks and handoff, Telegram is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: convert inbound messages into secure, idempotent tasks.


**Q39. [Interview] How do Slack and Discord interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. In this part of a multi-channel support bot with webhooks and handoff, Slack is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: convert inbound messages into secure, idempotent tasks. In this part of a multi-channel support bot with webhooks and handoff, Discord is an explicit design concern.


**Q40. [Interview] What would you do if WhatsApp fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure successful delivery and duplicate-trigger suppression.


**Q41. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for channel adapters?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q42. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M36.1?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the webhook event ledger and human-handoff transcript, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
