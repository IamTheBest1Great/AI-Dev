## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q49. [Interview] Explain business outcomes and unit economics to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Business outcomes and unit economics addresses the need to attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes. I would begin with a bounded design for a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, then specify contracts for Revenue and AI inference. I would measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure: an agent loop consumes credits through repeated unsuccessful searches.


**Q50. [Interview] How would you design Revenue from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. In this part of a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, Revenue is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes.


**Q51. [Interview] How do AI inference and infrastructure interact?**

**Answer:** Follow-up 1 — What would you test first? A negative case involving cost per task and a normal task with a verified outcome.


**Q52. [Interview] What would you do if tool/API costs fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature.


**Q53. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for business outcomes and unit economics?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q54. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M29.3?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the per-tenant cost ledger and accepted-outcome report, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
