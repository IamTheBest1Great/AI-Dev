## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q7. [Interview] Explain budget enforcement to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Budget enforcement addresses the need to attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes. I would begin with a bounded design for a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, then specify contracts for AI Platform & Infrastructure and Hard and soft limits. I would measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q8. [Interview] How would you design AI Platform & Infrastructure from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. In this part of a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, AI Platform & Infrastructure is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes.


**Q9. [Interview] How do Hard and soft limits and State and evidence interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. In this part of a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, Hard and soft limits is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes. Identify who owns this information, where it lives, and when it changes.


**Q10. [Interview] What would you do if Validation fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature.


**Q11. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for budget enforcement?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q12. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M29.5?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the per-tenant cost ledger and accepted-outcome report, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
