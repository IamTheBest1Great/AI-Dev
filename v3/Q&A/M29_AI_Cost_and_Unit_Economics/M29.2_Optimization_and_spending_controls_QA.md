## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q37. [Interview] Explain optimization and spending controls to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Optimization and spending controls addresses the need to attribute full operating cost to accepted business outcomes. I would begin with a bounded design for a multi-tenant AI product with model and tool usage, then specify contracts for Model routing and Prompt caching. I would measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q38. [Interview] How would you design Model routing from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. Selection of model/provider using task complexity, privacy, latency, cost and measured quality constraints. Then I would create at least a happy-path and an unauthorized/invalid-input test.


**Q39. [Interview] How do Prompt caching and Semantic caching interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Reuse provider-eligible repeated prompt prefixes or fragments. Benefits depend on exact payload, provider rules, TTL and isolation; it does not guarantee answer correctness. Reuse answers or retrieval results for similar requests after explicit thresholds and safety checks. Similarity alone is inadequate for tenant permissions or time-sensitive facts.


**Q40. [Interview] What would you do if Context trimming fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure total cost per successful task and margin per feature.


**Q41. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for optimization and spending controls?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q42. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M29.2?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the per-tenant cost ledger and accepted-outcome report, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
