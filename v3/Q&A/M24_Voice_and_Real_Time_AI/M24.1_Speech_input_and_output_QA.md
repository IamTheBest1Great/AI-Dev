**Answer:** Interview answer: Speech input and output addresses the need to manage low-latency conversations with interruption and human handoff. I would begin with a bounded design for a voice support and training assistant, then specify contracts for Transcription and Streaming transcription. **Q46. [Interview] How would you design Transcription from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. In this part of a voice support and training assistant, Transcription is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: manage low-latency conversations with interruption and human handoff.


**Q47. [Interview] How do Streaming transcription and Speaker diarization interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Process speech incrementally while retaining partial and final hypotheses; do not use unstable partial text for irreversible actions. Identify speaker turns or clusters in an audio recording; speaker labels are not verified identity claims. I would connect them only through a typed handoff with provenance and an explicit postcondition, rather than allowing hidden state coupling.


**Q48. [Interview] What would you do if Language detection fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure end-of-turn latency, completion rate and interruption recovery.


**Q49. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for speech input and output?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q50. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M24.1?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the conversation event log with latency and handoff markers, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
