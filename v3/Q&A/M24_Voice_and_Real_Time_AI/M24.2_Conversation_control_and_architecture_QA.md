**Q62. [Interview] How would you design Session state from scratch?**

Identify who owns this information, where it lives, and when it changes. Keep transient model context distinct from durable application state and enforce scope on every read and write. **Q63. [Interview] How do Turn detection and Interruptions interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. In this part of a voice support and training assistant, Turn detection is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: manage low-latency conversations with interruption and human handoff. In this part of a voice support and training assistant, Interruptions is an explicit design concern.


**Q64. [Interview] What would you do if Barge-in fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure end-of-turn latency, completion rate and interruption recovery.


**Q65. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for conversation control and architecture?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q66. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M24.2?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the conversation event log with latency and handoff markers, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
