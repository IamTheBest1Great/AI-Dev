## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q29. [Interview] Explain temporal media understanding to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Temporal media understanding addresses the need to ground claims in page images, tables, audio and video as available. I would begin with a bounded design for a multimodal maritime safety report analyzer, then specify contracts for Frame extraction and Keyframe selection. I would measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q30. [Interview] How would you design Frame extraction from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. Ground the operation in the current observable source and define how to verify it. Treat recognized or generated content as uncertain until checked against concrete evidence. Then I would create at least a happy-path and an unauthorized/invalid-input test.


**Q31. [Interview] How do Keyframe selection and Temporal sampling interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Choose representative video frames that capture meaningful changes without overwhelming the model with redundant near-identical images. Select frames at deliberate intervals or around events, balancing cost against risk of missing short important actions. I would connect them only through a typed handoff with provenance and an explicit postcondition, rather than allowing hidden state coupling.


**Q32. [Interview] What would you do if Scene segmentation fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness.


**Q33. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for temporal media understanding?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q34. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M23.2?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the multimodal evidence map with page/frame references, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
