## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q3. [Interview] Explain cross-modal quality to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Cross-modal quality addresses the need to ground claims in page images, tables, audio and video as available. I would begin with a bounded design for a multimodal maritime safety report analyzer, then specify contracts for Alignment and consistency and State and evidence. I would measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q4. [Interview] How would you design Alignment and consistency from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. In this part of a multimodal maritime safety report analyzer, Alignment and consistency is an explicit design concern. Define its inputs, owner, observable result and failure handling before implementing it. Its purpose must be linked to the overall goal: ground claims in page images, tables, audio and video as available.


**Q5. [Interview] How do State and evidence and Validation interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Identify who owns this information, where it lives, and when it changes. Keep transient model context distinct from durable application state and enforce scope on every read and write. Checking input and output data against schemas, required fields and domain constraints before further action. I would connect them only through a typed handoff with provenance and an explicit postcondition, rather than allowing hidden state coupling.


**Q6. [Interview] What would you do if Failure recovery fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness.


**Q7. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for cross-modal quality?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q8. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M23.6?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the multimodal evidence map with page/frame references, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
