## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q25. [Interview] Explain cross-modal representation and retrieval to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Follow-up 1 — What would you test first? A negative case involving page-image indexing and a normal task with a verified outcome.


**Q26. [Interview] How would you design Image embeddings from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. Represent images with vectors that support approximate similarity or cross-modal retrieval under an explicitly compatible model and evaluation contract. Then I would create at least a happy-path and an unauthorized/invalid-input test.


**Q27. [Interview] How do Text-image alignment and Cross-modal retrieval interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Ground the operation in the current observable source and define how to verify it. Treat recognized or generated content as uncertain until checked against concrete evidence. Search across text and images or other media with compatible aligned representations and filters; verify returned source evidence.


**Q28. [Interview] What would you do if Image metadata fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness.


**Q29. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for cross-modal representation and retrieval?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q30. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M23.4?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the multimodal evidence map with page/frame references, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
