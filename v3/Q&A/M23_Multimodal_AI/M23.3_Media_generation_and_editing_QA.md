## Cross-cutting production interview drill

These questions are adapted from the source study guide’s interview section and assess how the individual concepts work together.

**Q29. [Interview] Explain media generation and editing to an interviewer.**

**Answer:** Interview answer: Media generation and editing addresses the need to ground claims in page images, tables, audio and video as available. I would begin with a bounded design for a multimodal maritime safety report analyzer, then specify contracts for Text-to-image and Image editing. I would measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness, verify the actual outcome and demonstrate handling of the primary failure:.


**Q30. [Interview] How would you design Text-to-image from scratch?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would identify the trusted input source, decide which component owns the operation, define typed fields, and specify success and failure states. Ground the operation in the current observable source and define how to verify it. Treat recognized or generated content as uncertain until checked against concrete evidence. Then I would create at least a happy-path and an unauthorized/invalid-input test.


**Q31. [Interview] How do Image editing and Inpainting interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Ground the operation in the current observable source and define how to verify it. Treat recognized or generated content as uncertain until checked against concrete evidence. Replace or synthesize a chosen region of an image conditioned on surroundings; changes should be distinguishable from source evidence in investigative workflows.


**Q32. [Interview] What would you do if Outpainting fails in production?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. I would not blindly repeat a write. After fixing the cause, I would add the incident as a versioned regression case and measure visual grounding accuracy and source-attribution completeness.


**Q33. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for media generation and editing?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.


**Q34. [Interview] How would you demonstrate L3 production readiness for M23.3?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would present a working repository, a state and permission diagram, the multimodal evidence map with page/frame references, known limitations, representative evaluation fixtures, failure-injection results and tracing. I would also show restart behavior and an operator takeover path where needed.
