**Q103. [Interview] How do SLOs and Error budgets interact?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: First define their separate responsibilities. Service-level objectives are explicit reliability targets over a defined window and measurement method, such as 99% successful authorized tasks in 30 days. An allowance for SLO misses over a period that helps balance feature release velocity against reliability work. I would connect them only through a typed handoff with provenance and an explicit postcondition, rather than allowing hidden state coupling.

**Answer:** Interview answer: I would contain the impact, check trace IDs, state and external receipts, then classify the failure as transient, permanent or unknown outcome. **Q105. [Interview] When would you avoid a framework or model for recovery and operational learning?**

**Answer:** Interview answer: When the rules and data contracts are deterministic, a small application service or workflow is usually easier to test and operate. I add an agent or new framework only when variable inputs and evidence show a measurable improvement that offsets extra coordination, cost and risk.
