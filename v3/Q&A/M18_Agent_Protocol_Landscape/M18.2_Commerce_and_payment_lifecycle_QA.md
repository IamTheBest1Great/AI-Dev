## M18.2.1 — Agentic Commerce & Payments

**Q1. [Interview] What should an engineer understand about Agentic Commerce & Payments (M18.2.1)?**

**Answer:** This is the bridge between an autonomous agent's reasoning and real-world commercial action. The architecture must distinguish intent, authorization, transaction construction, execution, confirmation, and settlement.


**Q2. [Scenario] Why could overlooking Agentic Commerce & Payments create problems in a interoperable agent platform?**

**Answer:** In an agent commerce assistant with delegated authorization, this changes the decision criteria or the allowed workflow. Record who owns the rule, when it applies and how to verify compliance.
