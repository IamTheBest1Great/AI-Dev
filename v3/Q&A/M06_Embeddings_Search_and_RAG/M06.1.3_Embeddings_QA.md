**Q45. [Conceptual] Explain Dense vs Sparse Representations in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** An embedding model can produce a dense vector, but dense vectors are not the only way to represent text for retrieval. Dense and sparse describe the structure of a representation, not a simple old-versus-new technology split.


**Q46. [Interview] Why does Dense vs Sparse Representations matter, and what is one important design implication?**
