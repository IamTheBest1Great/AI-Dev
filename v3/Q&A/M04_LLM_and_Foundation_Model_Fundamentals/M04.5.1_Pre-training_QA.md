## 2. Deduplication

**Q11. [Conceptual] Explain Deduplication in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Deduplication is the process of identifying and removing duplicate or highly similar examples from a training corpus.


**Q12. [Interview] Why does Deduplication matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Detecting near-duplicates is more difficult because wording may change while meaning remains similar.

## 3. Tokenization

**Q13. [Conceptual] Explain Tokenization in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Tokenization is the process of converting text into smaller units called tokens, which are then represented using numerical token IDs for model processing.
