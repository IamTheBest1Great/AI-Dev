## 1. The Core Idea

**Q1. [Conceptual] Explain The Core Idea in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Task-based model selection is the process of choosing an AI model according to the specific requirements of an application rather than simply choosing the most powerful or popular model.


**Q2. [Interview] Why does The Core Idea matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** A model that generates excellent code may be expensive. A low-cost model may struggle to produce reliable structured JSON. A highly capable model may have greater latency. A vision-capable model may offer no advantage to an application that processes only text.

<a id="2-what-is-model-selection"></a>
## 2. What Is Model Selection?

**Q7. [Conceptual] What Is Model Selection?**

**Answer:** Model selection means evaluating available AI models and choosing one that best satisfies the functional and operational requirements of a particular use case.


**Q8. [Interview] How does Model Selection help a production system?**

**Answer:** A model can be technically capable of performing a task without being the most suitable choice for it.

## 4. Quality

**Q15. [Conceptual] Explain Quality in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Model quality is how good the model's final output is for the particular task being performed.

## 7. Structured Output Reliability

**Q41. [Conceptual] Explain Structured Output Reliability in simple, interview-ready language.**
