## 7. Limitations and Reward Hacking

**Q61. [Conceptual] Explain Limitations and Reward Hacking in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Preference- and reward-based training can improve behavior, but the signals used during training may be imperfect.


**Q62. [Interview] Why does Limitations and Reward Hacking matter, and what is one important design implication?**
