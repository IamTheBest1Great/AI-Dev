## 1. Compression and Knowledge Transfer

**Q1. [Conceptual] Explain Compression and Knowledge Transfer in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Compression and knowledge transfer are techniques used to make AI models smaller, faster, cheaper or more efficient while trying to preserve the useful capabilities of a larger or more capable model.


**Q2. [Interview] Why does Compression and Knowledge Transfer matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** The goal is not simply to reduce the model's size. It is to retain the capabilities that matter for the intended application while reducing the resources needed to run the model.


<a id="the-main-trade-off"></a>
### The main trade-off

**Q3. [Conceptual] Explain The main trade-off in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** The objective is to maximize useful capability retained per unit of computational cost.


**Q4. [Interview] Why does The main trade-off matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** For example, a company may not need its largest model to answer routine customer-support questions. A distilled model may be sufficient if it meets the application's quality requirements at a lower cost.
