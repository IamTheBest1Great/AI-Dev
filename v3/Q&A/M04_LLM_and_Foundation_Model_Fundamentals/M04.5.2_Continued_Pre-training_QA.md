## 1. Continued Pre-training

**Q1. [Conceptual] Explain Continued Pre-training in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Continued pre-training (CPT) is the process of taking an already pretrained model and training it further on additional data.


**Q2. [Interview] Why does Continued Pre-training matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Instead of initializing a model from scratch, we start with an existing base model whose parameters already contain learned patterns from earlier training.


<a id="2-domain-adaptive-corpora"></a>
## 2. Domain-Adaptive Corpora

**Q3. [Conceptual] Explain Domain-Adaptive Corpora in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** A domain is a specific field of knowledge or work.


**Q4. [Interview] Why does Domain-Adaptive Corpora matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Quality of the domain corpus. Quantity of useful training data. Diversity of the documents. Relevance of the data to the target application. Training configuration. Evaluation methodology.

**Q6. [Interview] Why does Additional Language Exposure matter, and what is one important design implication?**

<a id="4-retained-capabilities"></a>
## 4. Retained Capabilities

**Q7. [Conceptual] Explain Retained Capabilities in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Retained capabilities are the useful abilities a model already possesses and should preserve while learning new domain- or language-specific patterns.


**Q8. [Interview] Why does Retained Capabilities matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** The intended result is to improve the new domain without unnecessarily sacrificing useful existing abilities.


<a id="5-catastrophic-forgetting"></a>
## 5. Catastrophic Forgetting

**Q9. [Conceptual] Explain Catastrophic Forgetting in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** It is an important risk when adapting a general-purpose model to a narrow domain.


**Q10. [Interview] Why does Catastrophic Forgetting matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Catastrophic forgetting occurs when a model loses some previously learned capabilities as a result of training on new data.

## 6. Reducing Catastrophic Forgetting

**Q11. [Conceptual] Explain Reducing Catastrophic Forgetting in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Q12. [Interview] Why does Reducing Catastrophic Forgetting matter, and what is one important design implication?**
