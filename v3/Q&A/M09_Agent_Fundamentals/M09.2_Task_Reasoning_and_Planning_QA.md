### 1.2 Task Decomposition

**Q21. [Conceptual] Explain Task Decomposition in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Task decomposition breaks a larger objective into smaller, testable subtasks. A good subtask is independently verifiable and carries enough information for its successor.


**Q22. [Interview] Why does Task Decomposition matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Too broad → opaque failures and difficult retries. Too fine → excessive model calls, orchestration cost and latency. Decompose where there is a genuine dependency, independent check, permission boundary or recoverable failure boundary.
