## What Are Response Outcomes?

**Q1. [Conceptual] What Are Response Outcomes?**

**Answer:** After an AI model receives a request, the result is not necessarily just success or failure.


**Q2. [Interview] How would you justify the answer to “What Are Response Outcomes?” in an interview?**

**Answer:** These outcomes are different because each represents a different problem or result.

## Why Success vs. Failure Is Not Enough

**Q3. [Conceptual] Explain Why Success vs. Failure Is Not Enough in simple, interview-ready language.**

**Answer:** Suppose an AI assessment generator is asked to produce 10 multiple-choice questions.


**Q4. [Interview] Why does Why Success vs. Failure Is Not Enough matter, and what is one important design implication?**

**Answer:** Treating all these outcomes identically makes the application harder to debug and less reliable.
