## What Is Early Evaluation?

**Q1. [Conceptual] What Is Early Evaluation?**

**Answer:** Early evaluation means testing an AI system soon after the first model integration, before investing heavily in prompts, agents, application logic, infrastructure, caching, or production rollout.


**Q2. [Interview] How does Early Evaluation help a production system?**

**Answer:** Instead of assuming that a model will perform well and building a sophisticated system around it, test the basic model–task combination first.
